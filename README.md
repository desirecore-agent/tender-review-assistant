# Tender Review Assistant

A first-use entry for installing the verified tender review team and handing the user's goal and materials to its lead. Installation status and business quality are distinct; the assistant does not perform specialist review.

Use the platform market entry and actual installation records, reuse an existing matching team and follow current shared-rule approval controls. No draft bundled manifest or preparatory specialist runtime probes are required for normal handoff.

See the [entry guide](skills/tender-entry-bootstrap/README.md) and [中文](README.zh-CN.md). This outcome-oriented release does not establish real-tender accuracy or a new full business run; historical test materials were synthetic. Original content: [MIT](LICENSE), [NOTICE](NOTICE).
