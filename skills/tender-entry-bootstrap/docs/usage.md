# Usage

Preparation and how-to questions receive direct guidance. Asking “What materials should I prepare?” does not install a team or start a review. Installation runs only when requested or needed for an actionable task; the lead receives only actual review requests.

Describe what to review and provide authorized file/image locations. Example: “Check the tender and our bid for important qualification and pricing risks, with evidence and actionable corrections.” State any exclusions, deadlines or missing documents.

The assistant finds the verified market team, reuses or installs it through the platform, reports actual missing members or rule-review requirements, then transfers the request to the lead. Current market metadata supplies the version; this package does not maintain a second pinned installer manifest.

You receive a clear handoff status. The lead and specialists produce the substantive review; installation success alone says nothing about its accuracy. Prose or tables are normal outputs; request an attachment or structured export when useful.
